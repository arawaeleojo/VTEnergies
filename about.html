WHO WE ARE

Complete Value. Exceptional Service.

VROI-TREZ Energies (VT Energies) is a limited liability company, incorporated under the laws of the federal Republic of Nigeria with registered number RC7025466.

We specialize in procurement, Engineering, construction, Fabrication, Manpower Training and Development, Marine Equipment Supply, Scaffolding, Installation and Commissioning Services and Maintenance & Value Repair.

Our goal is to exceed the expectation of every client by offering standard, exceptional and value added services in terms of service reliability and a cost efficient alternative, whilst also complying with safe work procedure and other requisite standards.

We place premium on good company-community relationship, in other to reduce or eliminate subsidiary problems that may arise from our operations.

MISSION

To consistently deliver top-quality, innovative and cost-effective services to our clients through value-adding strategies and ensure strict adherence to safe work practices.

VISION

To create a dependable global name in the Oil & Gas, Marine and Energy industry through the ues of cutting edge technology, equipment, and a well-experienced workforce.
